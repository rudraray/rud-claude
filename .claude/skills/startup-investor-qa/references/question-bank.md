# Core question bank

Fourteen questions in the style of accelerator and early-stage investor interviews. For each: what the interviewer is testing, what a strong answer contains, the trap, likely follow-ups, and where the material lives in the venture folder.

The "what it tests" notes are reasoned guidance, not statements from any particular investor. Adjust to the audience.

## 1. What are you building? Be specific.

- **Tests:** whether the founder can state the product plainly. Vague answers suggest a vague product.
- **Strong answer:** one sentence with the product type, the specific customer, and what it does for them. A stranger could repeat it.
- **Trap:** category words ("platform", "ecosystem", "AI-powered solution"), vision before product, or a list of features.
- **Follow-ups:** "So it's a...?" / "Who exactly is the user?" / "What does it replace?"
- **Source:** `07-pitch.md` one sentence; `00-idea-brief.md`.

## 2. Pick one customer. Walk us through how they use your product.

- **Tests:** whether a real customer exists and the founder has watched them use it.
- **Strong answer:** a named or clearly described real customer (with permission to name), the moment they open the product, the steps they take, what comes out, how often. If no customer uses it yet, say so and walk through the pilot or the observed manual process instead.
- **Trap:** describing an imagined average user, or a feature tour. Inventing a customer.
- **Follow-ups:** "How often do they do that?" / "What did they do before?" / "Who else in the company touches it?"
- **Source:** `01-icp.md` profile; interview notes; `evidence-log.md`.

## 3. What does your product actually do for them?

- **Tests:** whether the benefit is concrete and measurable, as the customer would describe it.
- **Strong answer:** the before and after for one customer in time, money, errors, or risk, with the measurement and where it came from. If not yet measured, give the customer's own estimate and say it is an estimate.
- **Trap:** repeating features; unsourced percentages.
- **Follow-ups:** "How do you know?" / "Would they say the same?" / "What happens if they stop using it?"
- **Source:** `01-icp.md` outcome; `evidence-log.md`.

## 4. Are people using it yet?

- **Tests:** real traction, and honesty about it.
- **Strong answer:** yes or no first. Then counts: how many active, how often, since when, retention so far. If no: what exists (pilots agreed, waitlist, date of first use) and what is blocking.
- **Trap:** counting sign-ups as users; "lots of interest"; answering a different question.
- **Follow-ups:** "How many used it last week?" / "How many stopped?" / "Why did they stop?"
- **Source:** founder's current numbers; `evidence-log.md`.

## 5. How much are they paying?

- **Tests:** willingness to pay, and whether pricing has been tested.
- **Strong answer:** the actual price and unit (per month, per seat), number of paying customers, total monthly revenue. If unpaid: say "nothing yet", then what has been asked for and the response (price quoted, letters of intent, paid pilot agreed).
- **Trap:** quoting list price when nobody pays it; treating free pilots as revenue; projected revenue in answer to a present-tense question.
- **Follow-ups:** "Why that price?" / "Has anyone said no on price?" / "What would they pay next year?"
- **Source:** `02-business-plan.md` business model; `04-financial-model/` drivers.

## 6. We see this idea a lot. Why will you be the one to crack it?

- **Tests:** awareness of competitors and earlier failures, and a real edge.
- **Strong answer:** acknowledge others exist; name what they got wrong or whom they serve badly; state the specific insight, access, or ability this team has, with the evidence for it (experience in the industry, an early result, a distribution route).
- **Trap:** "no competitors"; "we execute better"; "our technology is better" with no proof; dismissing rivals.
- **Follow-ups:** "Why hasn't the big incumbent done this?" / "What do you know that they don't?" / "Why did the last company fail?"
- **Source:** `00-idea-brief.md` why us; `02-business-plan.md` alternatives; `05-partnerships.md`.

## 7. Aren't you just building custom software for every customer?

- **Tests:** whether this is a scalable product or a services business in disguise.
- **Strong answer:** an honest split: what share of the product is the same for every customer, what is configured, what is bespoke. If much is bespoke today, say so, explain why it is deliberate at this stage (learning what to standardise), and give the evidence it is converging: the second customer took less work than the first, with numbers.
- **Trap:** flat denial when the files show heavy per-customer work; no data on onboarding effort.
- **Follow-ups:** "How long did the last onboarding take?" / "What did you build for one customer that nobody else uses?" / "What is your gross margin with that work included?"
- **Source:** `02-business-plan.md` product; `04-financial-model/` gross margin; `03-phased-roadmap.md`.

## 8. What will you get done by demo day?

- **Tests:** whether the founder sets concrete, ambitious, measurable short-term goals. Substitute the relevant deadline (end of programme, next meeting, three months).
- **Strong answer:** one to three measurable outcomes with numbers and dates (paying customers, revenue, a launch), the weekly activity that gets there, and current pace. These should match the next gate in the roadmap.
- **Trap:** activity goals ("keep building", "talk to more customers"); targets with no path from today's numbers.
- **Follow-ups:** "What's the number today?" / "What has to go right?" / "What will you cut if you fall behind?"
- **Source:** `03-phased-roadmap.md` current phase gate; `02-business-plan.md` 90-day goals.

## 9. Who does what on the team?

- **Tests:** clear ownership, the ability to build and sell without outsiders, and how the founders work together.
- **Strong answer:** each person's role in a phrase, who builds, who sells, who decides; how long they have worked together; full-time or not; the gap and how it will be filled.
- **Trap:** titles without responsibilities; hiding a part-time founder or an outsourced build.
- **Follow-ups:** "Who wrote the code?" / "Is everyone full-time?" / "How did you meet?" / "Who has the final say?"
- **Source:** `00-idea-brief.md` team; `02-business-plan.md` team.

## 10. How many customers have you spoken to?

- **Tests:** depth of customer discovery and what was learned.
- **Strong answer:** the real count, who they were (roles, company types), and the one or two things learned that changed the product or the target customer. A small honest number with a clear lesson is better than a large vague one.
- **Trap:** rounding up; counting casual chats or survey responses as interviews; no lesson.
- **Follow-ups:** "What surprised you?" / "How many said they'd pay?" / "How did you find them?" / "What did the ones who said no say?"
- **Source:** `01-icp.md` interview plan and results; `evidence-log.md`.

## 11. How are you selling?

- **Tests:** whether there is a working route to customers and the founders do the selling themselves.
- **Strong answer:** the actual motion today: how leads are found, who contacts them, the steps to a yes, how long it takes, and conversion numbers so far, however small (for example: 50 contacted, 12 calls, 3 pilots).
- **Trap:** a list of future channels ("SEO, partnerships, virality"); no numbers; plans to hire a salesperson before the founders have sold.
- **Follow-ups:** "Who closed the last deal?" / "How long did it take?" / "What does it cost you to get one customer?" / "Will that work for the next hundred?"
- **Source:** `02-business-plan.md` go-to-market; `05-partnerships.md`; `04-financial-model/` CAC.

## 12. What's actually built? Show us.

- **Tests:** real progress and the ability to ship.
- **Strong answer:** state what is live, what is prototype, what is still manual behind the scenes, and what is mock-up; then a 60 to 90 second demo of the main thing a customer does. Prepare the demo: one path, real or realistic data, a recorded fallback in case it fails.
- **Trap:** presenting mock-ups as working product; a long demo of settings and login screens; no fallback.
- **Follow-ups:** "Is that live data?" / "How long did it take to build?" / "What breaks at ten times the users?" / "What's next?"
- **Source:** founder's current build status; `03-phased-roadmap.md`.

## 13. Will your first customers actually use it?

- **Tests:** whether stated interest will turn into regular use, and the evidence for that.
- **Strong answer:** evidence of commitment in descending strength: already using it regularly, paid, signed a pilot with dates, gave data or staff time, or only said they are interested. Say which level each early customer is at, and what will be done in the first weeks to make sure use happens.
- **Trap:** treating enthusiasm, letters of intent, or a friendly contact as proof of use.
- **Follow-ups:** "What have they committed so far?" / "Who inside the customer has to change their routine?" / "What happened with the last pilot?"
- **Source:** `01-icp.md`; `evidence-log.md`; `03-phased-roadmap.md` gate evidence.

## 14. Where do they need it most?

- **Tests:** whether the founder knows the sharpest point of pain: which customer segment, and which moment in their work. This shows focus.
- **Strong answer:** the specific segment and the specific situation where the problem is most costly or frequent, how the founder knows (what customers said or did), and that this is where the product starts.
- **Trap:** "everyone needs it" or a list of many use cases.
- **Follow-ups:** "Why there and not elsewhere?" / "How big is just that piece?" / "Where do you go after that?"
- **Source:** `01-icp.md` chosen beachhead, triggers, scores.
